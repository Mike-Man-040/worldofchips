Potato Chips in Poland

Europe

Ranked fifth in the world for potato consumption (FAOSTAT/Kantar Public data cited by Lorenz), Poland is one of Central Europe's biggest potato-chip producers as well as an avid snacking market. PepsiCo launched its Polish potato-farming programme in 1993 and makes Lay's at its plant in Grodzisk Mazowiecki near Warsaw — one of PepsiCo's largest chip factories in Europe, contracting around 240,000 tonnes of Polish potatoes a year from roughly 70 farms and processing more than 750 tonnes of tubers a day across four lines (up to 4 tonnes of finished chips an hour). Germany's Lorenz Bahlsen Snack-World is the #2 manufacturer with Crunchips and Wiejskie Ziemniaczki ("Country Potatoes"), an unpeeled chip made exclusively from over 10 varieties of Polish potatoes stored at Stanowice in Lower Silesia and sourced from nearly 100 growers across Lower Silesia, Opole and Greater Poland; Lorenz notes that each potato goes from washing to a sealed bag in just 45 minutes across 10 steps. Intersnack Poland, with production in Nysa, competes with Przysnacki (launched around 2012), while discount leader Biedronka backs its private-label Top Chips. Polish flavour preferences lean heavily toward herbs, dairy and forest mushrooms: Zielona Cebulka (Green Onion), Papryka and creamy Fromage dominate everyday sales, while Wiejskie Ziemniaczki and Przysnacki champion rustic Polish tastes like Masło z Solą (Butter & Salt), Koperek (Dill), Wędzonka (Smoked Bacon) and Kurki w Śmietanie (Chanterelles in Cream).

Top Potato Chip Brands in Poland

According to Listonic and Moja Gazetka shopping-list market-share data (September–October 2026), Lay's leads Poland's chips, crisps, popcorn and nachos category with a 42.46% share, ahead of Lorenz's Crunchips (13.85%), Pringles (9.91%), Intersnack's Przysnacki (7.66%), Biedronka's private-label Top Chips (7.17%), Cheetos (6.95%) and Lorenz's unpeeled Polish-potato brand Wiejskie Ziemniaczki (4.29%–5.66%).

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Poland, potato chips are the go-to sharing snack for domówki (house parties), Champions League and national-team football broadcasts, and summer grillowanie (barbecues). Creamy, herb-forward flavours — Zielona Cebulka (spring onion), Fromage, Koperek (dill) and Papryka — are paired with cold beer or served in big bowls alongside paluszki (salted pretzel sticks).

FAQ: Potato Chips in Poland

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Poland?

The leading potato chip brands in Poland are Lay's, Crunchips, Pringles, Przysnacki and Wiejskie Ziemniaczki, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about 8 PLN/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Poland?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Poland are Zielona Cebulka (Green Onion), Paprika, Fromage and Salted. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Kurki w Śmietanie (Chanterelles), Koperek (Dill), Masło z Solą (Butter & Salt) and Wędzonka (Smoked Bacon). Chips are typically eaten during football match viewing & grilling (grillowanie), domówka house parties and evening TV & student gatherings.
