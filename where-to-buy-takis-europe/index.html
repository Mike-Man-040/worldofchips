🇩🇪 Takis in Germany

In Germany Takis Fuego is sold as “Takis Fuego Extreme 100g”. REWE lists it in its online shop, and shoppers on the price tracker Supermarktcheck have reported it at REWE, EDEKA and Globus for €2.99. The deal tracker Aktionspreis.de gives €2.99 as the recommended retail price, with promotions as low as €1.99 in recent months.

  • REWE: Takis Fuego Extreme 100 g, in the online shop and in stores.
  • EDEKA and Globus: reported at €2.99 by Supermarktcheck users; range varies by store.

Sources: www.rewe.de/shop/p/takis-fuego-extreme-100g/9019559, www.supermarktcheck.de/product/455196-takis-fuego-extreme-100g, www.aktionspreis.de/sorte/takis-fuego-100g-angebote

FAQs

Can you buy Takis in the Netherlands?

Yes. Albert Heijn, Jumbo and PLUS listed Takis Fuego 100 g at €2.99 when we checked on 3 November 2026, and Jumbo also listed Blue Heat, Intense Nacho and Volcano. SPAR listed Fuego at €3.49.

Where can I buy Takis in Germany?

REWE sells Takis Fuego Extreme 100 g, and price-tracker users have reported it at EDEKA and Globus for €2.99. Promotions sometimes bring it down to about €1.99.

Are Takis sold in Swedish supermarkets?

We did not find them in the online range of Hemköp when we checked. Online candy shops such as Coopers Candy, Godisjätten and Delitea listed Takis Fuego for about 35–43 kr per 100 g bag.
