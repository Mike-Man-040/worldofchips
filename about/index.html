About World of Chips

Last checked 27 October 2026

A guide to potato chips around the world: brands, flavours, prices and where to buy them.

World of Chips covers potato chips (crisps, not fries) country by country: the brands people actually buy, the flavours that only exist in one place, and where to get them online.

Who Runs World of Chips

Michael van der Looij is the founder of World of Chips. He has a background in finance and writes and checks the guides, including the Pringles Index price comparison.

How We Work

  • Facts and figures link to their sources, and each guide shows the date it was last checked.
  • Where we can’t find a reliable source, we say so instead of guessing.
