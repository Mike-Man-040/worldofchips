FAQs

What is the most popular chip brand in Mexico?

Sabritas, founded in Mexico City in 1943 and part of PepsiCo since 1966, is Mexico’s original chip brand. Its main rival is Barcel, Grupo Bimbo’s snack arm, which makes Takis and Chip’s.

Are Takis Mexican?

Yes. Takis are made by Barcel, part of Mexico’s Grupo Bimbo. They were invented in 1999, first sold as “Taquis”, and the purple-bag Fuego (chilli and lime) arrived in 2006.

What flavours are typical of Mexican chips?

Lime, salt and chile above all, often with chamoy or tamarind. In Mexico, chips are often “prepared” with hot sauce, lime and toppings rather than eaten plain.
