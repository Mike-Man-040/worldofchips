FAQs

How does the chip quiz work?

Each answer adds points to one or more countries, and the country with the most points is your result. The scoring is our own light-hearted editorial mapping, not a scientific test.

Can I share my quiz result?

Yes. Your result gets its own link (for example worldofchips.com/which-chip-are-you/?r=japan) that opens straight to the same result.
