Potato Chips in Thailand

Asia

Thailand's chip aisle has been a two-brand contest for three decades. PepsiCo has sold Lay's in Thailand since 1995 and opened its first Thai snack factory in Lamphun province. Its local rival, Tasto, went on sale in 1996, after the Bangkok conglomerate Berli Jucker (BJC) bought Siam Snack, a former joint venture with PepsiCo, and launched a chip brand of its own. In 2008 Positioning magazine put Lay's at 60% of Thai potato-chip sales and Tasto at 26%, with brands such as Koikeya's Karamucho sharing the rest. By April 2025 Gotomanager valued the potato-chip segment at more than ฿15 billion, inside a snack market worth over ฿47 billion. The flavours are what make Thai chips famous. Seaweed is a staple, from Lay's Nori Seaweed to Tasto's Japanese Seaweed, and Tasto's signature Crab Curry, Japanese Seaweed and BBQ Max together made up more than 70% of its sales in 2008. Other bags turn Thai dishes into seasoning: Lay's Rock Hot Chili Squid, Salted Egg, Miang Kham and Larb Tod, or Tasto's Pla Sam Rod (sweet, sour and spicy fried fish). Most chips are bought at the country's 15,945 7-Eleven stores (CP All, end of 2025), at supermarkets such as Big C and Lotus's, and at traditional corner shops.

Top Potato Chip Brands in Thailand

PepsiCo's Lay's (sold in Thailand since 1995, including the ridged Lay's Rock line) leads the Thai potato-chip market. Its main local challenger is Berli Jucker Foods' Tasto (on sale since 1996), which comes in flat, ridge and waffle cuts, while Koikeya's Japanese Karamucho brand is among the smaller players.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Thailand, chips are an impulse buy. In 2008 Tasto's best-selling size was a 15 g bag for 5 baht, sold mostly through traditional shops, and today the 7-Eleven found on almost every Bangkok street sells chips around the clock. Flavours based on Thai dishes, like Crab Curry, Miang Kham and Pla Sam Rod, also make bags of chips a popular take-home souvenir for visitors.

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Thailand?

The leading potato chip brands in Thailand are Lay's and Tasto, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about ฿35/bag.
