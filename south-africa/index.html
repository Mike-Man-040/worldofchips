What are the most popular potato chip brands in South Africa?

The leading potato chip brands in South Africa are Simba, Lay's, Willards and NikNaks, with Simba at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. South Africa's chip market is listed at $0.5B, with an average bag price of R25/bag.
