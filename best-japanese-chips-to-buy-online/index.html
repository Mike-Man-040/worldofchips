FAQs

Which Japanese chips should I try first?

Start with Calbee’s three classics: Usushio (1975), Norishio (1976) and Consommé Punch (1978). Add Koikeya Karamucho (1984) if you like heat.

Who makes the most popular Japanese chips?

Calbee is Japan’s best-known potato chip maker. Koikeya is the other big name and was the first company in Japan to mass-produce potato chips.
