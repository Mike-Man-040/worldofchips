Potato Chips in Turkey

Asia

Turkey came late to packaged chips. According to the Turkish Competition Authority, industrial chip production only began in 1986, when Uzay Gıda invested in the category, and for about seven years it was effectively the country's only producer. PepsiCo bought half of Uzay Gıda in 1988 and the rest in 1993; renamed Frito Lay Gıda in 1999, it sells Lay's, Ruffles, Doritos, Cheetos and the corn snack Çerezza. Competition arrived in 1994, when Kar Gıda entered the market, and Pringles followed as an import in 1998. Kraft bought Kar Gıda and its Patos, Cipso and Çerezos brands in 2002, and in 2013 Mondelez sold the business to Doğuş Çay. Turkey's signature chip flavour is yoghurt with seasonal herbs (yoğurt ve mevsim yeşillikli). Lay's says its version has been on the market for a long time, and rivals such as Cipso sell their own. Ketchup (ketçaplı) chips, especially from Ruffles, and spiced (baharatlı) chips are the other staples. Shoppers buy them everywhere from supermarkets to discount chains such as BİM, where a 125 g bag of Lay's was listed at ₺64.50 in October 2026.

Top Potato Chip Brands in Turkey

Two groups lead Turkey's chip aisle. PepsiCo's Frito Lay Türkiye, the former Uzay Gıda, sells Lay's, Ruffles, Doritos and Cheetos plus the Çerezza corn snack. Doğuş Çay owns Patos, Cipso and Çerezos, which it bought from Mondelez in 2013. Pringles has been sold in Turkey since 1998.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

Turkish chip shelves lean towards bold seasonings. Alongside yoghurt and herbs and ketchup you'll find Ruffles' grilled-meat flavour (Mangalda Et) and Taco spice, Lay's spiced chips with strained cheese (Baharatlı ve Süzme Peynirli), and Doritos Turca. Bags range from small single packs to 150-200 g party sizes (parti boy and mega boy).

FAQ: Potato Chips in Turkey

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Turkey?

The leading potato chip brands in Turkey are Lay's, Ruffles, Doritos, Patos, Cipso and Pringles, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about ₺65/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Turkey?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Turkey are Klasik (Classic), Yoğurt ve Mevsim Yeşillikli (Yoghurt & Herbs), Baharatlı (Spiced) and Ketçaplı (Ketchup). For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Yoğurt ve Mevsim Yeşillikli (Yoghurt & Seasonal Herbs), Ruffles Ketçap (Ketchup), Ruffles Mangalda Et (Grilled Meat) and Lay's Baharatlı ve Süzme Peynirli (Spiced & Strained Cheese). Chips are typically eaten during football viewing, picnics and late-night snacking.
