Air-Fryer Crisps in Brief

  • Slice thinly with a mandoline and soak the slices in ice-cold water for 15 minutes.
  • Pat them completely dry. BBC Good Food notes they won’t crisp if even slightly wet.
  • Toss with a little oil and seasoning, then cook in a single layer at 210 °C for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway.

FAQs

Can you make crisps in an air fryer?

Yes. BBC Good Food’s method soaks thin slices in ice-cold water for 15 minutes, dries them thoroughly and tosses them with a little oil and seasoning. They cook at 210 °C for 12 to 15 minutes, shaken halfway.

Why are my air-fryer crisps soggy?

Usually the slices were too thick, still wet or overlapping. Slice thinly, dry them well and cook in a single layer, in batches if needed.

Which air fryer size is best for chips?

A larger basket fits more slices in one layer, which means fewer batches. Dual-basket models let you run two batches at once.
