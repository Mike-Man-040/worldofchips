Britain gave the world the flavoured crisp. In 1954 Ireland’s Tayto created cheese & onion, and Walkers launched its own Cheese & Onion the same year. Walkers was founded in Leicester in 1948 and is now Britain’s largest crisp maker. Its Salt & Shake revives the old Smith’s idea of a twist of salt in the bag. FAQs

What are the most popular British crisp flavours?

Walkers’ big three are Ready Salted, Cheese & Onion (since 1954) and Salt & Vinegar (since 1967, a nod to fish and chips).

What is the biggest crisp brand in the UK?

Walkers, founded in Leicester in 1948 and owned by PepsiCo since 1989, is Britain’s largest crisp maker. Tyrrell’s, Pipers, Kettle and McCoy’s are other well-known names.

Who invented cheese and onion crisps?

Cheese & onion is widely credited to Joe “Spud” Murphy of Ireland’s Tayto and his employee Seamus Burke in 1954. Walkers launched its own Cheese & Onion the same year.
