Potato Chips in Indonesia

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Indonesia, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during warung snacking. Other popular snacking occasions include gatherings, late-night gaming, school canteen. The average bag of chips costs Rp 10k/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Indonesia

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Indonesia?

The leading potato chip brands in Indonesia are Lay's, Chitato, Pringles and Qtela, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. Indonesia's chip market is listed at $0.8B, with an average bag price of Rp 10k/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Indonesia?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Indonesia are Original, BBQ, Cheese and Sapi Panggang. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Rendang, Indomie Goreng, Sambal Matah and Nasi Goreng. Chips are typically eaten during warung snacking, gatherings and late-night gaming.
