Potato Chips in Indonesia

Asia

Indonesia's potato chip market is valued at $0.8B and grows at 7.5% per year. With per capita consumption of 0.2 kg and an average bag price of Rp 10k/bag, Indonesia has developed its own distinctive chip culture shaped by local culinary traditions and snacking habits. The market features both international giants and beloved local brands, each offering flavors that reflect the country's unique food heritage. From Original to the more adventurous Rendang, Indonesia's chip scene offers a fascinating window into local taste preferences and snacking culture.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Indonesia, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during warung snacking. Other popular snacking occasions include gatherings, late-night gaming, school canteen. The average bag of chips costs Rp 10k/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

  • Warung snacking
  • Gatherings
  • Late-night gaming
  • School canteen
