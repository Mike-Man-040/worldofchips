Potato Chips in Indonesia

Asia

Indonesia's best-known potato chip is Chitato, Indofood's wavy-cut brand. Until 1992 it was sold under the Chiki name. That year it became an independent brand with a Japanese-style logo and a mascot called "The Real-Potato King", and it came in just three flavours, each with its own packaging colour: plain salt, spicy chicken and beef barbecue. Chitato is made by Indofood's snack subsidiaries, and its most famous flavour came from a sister brand. In 2016 Indofood released a limited-edition Chitato flavoured like Indomie Mi Goreng, the country's favourite instant fried noodles. Bags reached Jakarta stores in mid-February 2016 and sold out fast. After a social-media petition (#BalikinChitatoIndomie), Indofood brought the Mi Goreng flavour back nationwide in April 2022, selling it through local shops, minimarkets, hypermarkets and supermarkets.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Indonesia

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Indonesia?

The leading potato chip brands in Indonesia are Chitato, Lay's and Pringles, with Chitato at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about Rp 10k/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Indonesia?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Indonesia are Plain Salt, Spicy Chicken and Beef Barbecue. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Mi Goreng (Indomie). Chips are typically eaten during warung snacking, gatherings and late-night gaming.
