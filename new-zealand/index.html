Potato Chips in New Zealand

Oceania

New Zealand's favourite chip brand started at the speedway. In 1953 Les Saussey began cooking sliced potatoes in a converted wash house on Khyber Pass Road in Auckland and selling them, with popcorn, at the Western Springs Speedway. Bluebird opened its doors that year, and he soon added Salt & Vinegar and Chicken flavours, at 9 pence a packet. Its 1960s TV jingle, "Bird, bird, bird, Bluebird's the word", is still widely remembered, and today Bluebird is PepsiCo's New Zealand snack arm, also making Twisties, Cheezels, Burger Rings and Doritos. Its main rival is Griffin's, which sells Eta (including Ripples), Kettle and Proper Crisps. Proper Crisps was founded in 2007 by English couple Stuart and Kathryn Franklin at Upper Moutere near Nelson and bought by Griffin's in 2023. The most Kiwi way to eat plain chips is with onion dip made from reduced cream and onion soup mix, and Bluebird sells a Kiwi Onion Dip chip flavour. It also plays with local tastes: its limited 2020 Kiwi Flavourites range included Paua Fritters and Lemon, Lamington, Cheese and Onion Toastie, and Sunday Roast. Chips are sold in supermarkets, petrol stations and convenience stores, and a 150 g bag of Bluebird Original Cut normally costs about NZ$3.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In New Zealand, a bowl of chips with onion dip is a fixture at parties and barbecues. The dip, made by mixing reduced cream with onion soup mix, was created in the early 1960s in Nestlé's test kitchen, and Bluebird's senior brand manager has named Salt & Vinegar with Kiwi onion dip as her favourite combination. Chips are sold in supermarkets, petrol stations and convenience stores.

Did You Know?

Kiwi onion dip, the classic partner for plain chips, was created in the early 1960s by Rosemary Dempsey, head of Nestlé's New Zealand test kitchen, who mixed reduced cream with onion soup mix to rescue slow-selling onion soup.

FAQ: Potato Chips in New Zealand

What are the most popular potato chip brands in New Zealand?

The leading potato chip brands in New Zealand are Bluebird, Eta, Proper Crisps and Kettle, with Bluebird at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about NZ$3/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in New Zealand?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in New Zealand are Ready Salted, Salt & Vinegar, Chicken and Sour Cream & Chives. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Kiwi Onion Dip, Green Onion, Paua Fritters & Lemon (2020 limited edition) and Lamington (2020 limited edition). Chips are typically eaten during chips & onion dip at parties, barbecues and watching sport.
