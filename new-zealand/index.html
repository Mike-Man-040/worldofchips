Potato Chips in New Zealand

Oceania

New Zealand's potato chip market is valued at $0.3B and grows at 2.0% per year. With per capita consumption of 2.3 kg and an average bag price of NZ$5/bag, New Zealand has developed its own distinctive chip culture shaped by local culinary traditions and snacking habits. The market features both international giants and beloved local brands, each offering flavors that reflect the country's unique food heritage. From Ready Salted to the more adventurous Kiwi Onion Dip, New Zealand's chip scene offers a fascinating window into local taste preferences and snacking culture.

FAQ: Potato Chips in New Zealand

What are the most popular potato chip brands in New Zealand?

The leading potato chip brands in New Zealand are Bluebird, Kettle, Proper Crisps and ETA, with Bluebird at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. New Zealand's chip market is listed at $0.3B, with an average bag price of NZ$5/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in New Zealand?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in New Zealand are Ready Salted, Salt & Vinegar, Chicken and Sour Cream & Chives. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Kiwi Onion Dip, Marmite, Hokey Pokey and L&P.
