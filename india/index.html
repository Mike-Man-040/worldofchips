Potato Chips in India

Asia

Branded potato chips in India date back to 1992, when Amrit Agro launched Uncle Chipps, a pioneer of the category famous for its jingle "Bole mere lips, I love Uncle Chipps". PepsiCo brought Lay's to India in 1995, sold small packs from ₹5 to ₹25 and won shoppers over with local flavours, above all India's Magic Masala, the spicy, tangy "blue Lay's". In October 2000 it bought Uncle Chipps. ITC entered in March 2007 with Bingo!, whose launch range put potato chips alongside new finger snacks such as the khakra-inspired Mad Angles and Tedhe Medhe. The biggest homegrown success is Balaji Wafers. The Virani brothers started out running the canteen at Rajkot's Astron Cinema in the 1970s and began making their own wafers in 1982. By 2023 the Gujarat company was the third-largest player in India's ₹43,800-crore salty-snacks market, with a 12% share behind Haldiram's (21%) and PepsiCo (15%), according to The Economic Times. In January 2026 the US investor General Atlantic bought a stake of about 7%, at a valuation reported at roughly ₹35,000 crore (about $4 billion). Indian flavours lean heavily on masala. Balaji's range includes Masala Masti, Chaat Chaska, Pudina Twist and Tomato Twist, while Lay's sells Magic Masala next to Spanish Tomato Tango and American Style Cream & Onion.

Top Potato Chip Brands in India

PepsiCo's Lay's (in India since 1995) and Uncle Chipps (launched in 1992, bought by PepsiCo in 2000) compete with Rajkot's Balaji Wafers, the leader in Gujarat, Maharashtra and Rajasthan. ITC's Bingo! (2007) and Haldiram's, founded in Bikaner in 1937 and best known for bhujia and namkeen, are the other major names.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In India, chips sell in small, cheap packs. Lay's built its market on ₹5 to ₹25 bags, school canteens sell them for pocket money, and a bag of Magic Masala is a fixture at cricket matches and birthday parties. Today they are also delivered within minutes by quick-commerce apps such as Zepto.

FAQ: Potato Chips in India

What are the most popular potato chip brands in India?

The leading potato chip brands in India are Lay's, Balaji, Haldiram's, Bingo! and Uncle Chipps, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about ₹20/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in India?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in India are India's Magic Masala, Classic Salted, American Style Cream & Onion and Masala Masti. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Spanish Tomato Tango, Chaat Chaska, Pudina (Mint) Twist and Achaari Masti (Mango Pickle). Chips are typically eaten during cricket match viewing, chai-time snacking and birthday parties and festivals.
