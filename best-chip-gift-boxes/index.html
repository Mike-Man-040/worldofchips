FAQs

What is a good gift for someone who loves chips?

For a dressed-up present, a ready-to-gift box such as the Broadway Basketeers tower (six stacked boxes with chips, popcorn and cookies) works well. For a pure chip fan, an imported multipack, like Australian Red Rock Deli or British Monster Munch, offers flavours they probably can’t buy locally.

Can I add a gift message on Amazon?

The Ultimate Variety Sampler listing says gift wrapping and a message are available at checkout. The Broadway Basketeers tower comes with a gift tag. For other items, check the gift options shown at checkout.

Are there chip gift boxes with international flavours?

Yes. Imported multipacks such as Red Rock Deli (Australia) and Walkers Monster Munch (UK) are sold on Amazon.com. For a monthly box of Japanese or Korean snacks, see our snack box comparison.

What is the difference between a gift box and a variety pack?

A gift box is packaged to be given, often with mixed sweet and savoury snacks and a tag or message. A variety pack is a bulk box of single-serve chip bags, better value for offices, parties or stocking a pantry, but plainly packaged.
