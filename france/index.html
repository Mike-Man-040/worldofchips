Potato Chips in France

Europe

France's potato-chip market has been transformed over the past decade by a boom in flavoured, regional and apéritif chips. According to Circana figures reported by LSA and RMC/BFMTV in April 2025, French chip consumption rose 12.5% in volume after the pandemic and 42% over ten years, while the average supermarket aisle expanded from 108 to 120 chip references in five years. Historically, French industrial chip making goes back to 1955, when potato growers in Vic-sur-Aisne (Picardy) founded the Vico cooperative; Vico joined Germany's Intersnack group in 2007. Today, PepsiCo's Lay's leads in value market share at 26.5% (with Nature, Paysanne, Ancienne, Poulet Rôti and Emmental), closely chased at 23% by Bret's, the flagship brand of family-owned Breton producer Altho. Founded in 1995 by Alain Glon in Saint-Gérand-Croixanvec (Morbihan, Brittany), Altho cooks French potatoes in sunflower oil and has turned the chip aisle into a tour of French gastronomy — from Chèvre & Piment d'Espelette, Comté, Camembert and Tartiflette to Poulet Braisé, Cèpes (porcini) and Sel de Guérande. Intersnack France (Vico and Tyrrell's) holds 17.4% value share, southern French maker Sibell (La Ciotat) represents Provençal craft chips, and supermarket private labels account for roughly 30% of the market.

Top Potato Chip Brands in France

According to Circana data reported by LSA and RMC/BFMTV (2025), PepsiCo's Lay's leads France's potato-chip market at 26.5% value share, followed by Brittany's family-owned Bret's (Altho, founded 1995) at 23% and Intersnack France (Vico, founded 1955, plus Tyrrell's) at 17.4%, while supermarket private labels hold around 30% (and dominate plain salted chips). In the fast-growing tortilla-chip (chips tortilla / nachos) aisle at E.Leclerc, Carrefour, Intermarché and Auchan, PepsiCo's Doritos (Nacho Cheese, Sweet Chilli Pepper, Cool Original and Nature, typically €1.50–€2.11 for 160 g or €2.69–€3.45 for 250 g, roughly €9.40–€13.20/kg) and General Mills' Old El Paso tortilla chips (around €2.24–€2.83 for 185 g, or €12.10–€15.30/kg) lead branded sales, while organic bags from Jardin Bio Étic and retailer own-labels (Marque Repère, Carrefour, Chabrior, Lidl Snack Day) sell for 30%–45% less per kilo.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In France, chips belong first and foremost to l'heure de l'apéro — the pre-dinner gathering around wine, pastis, beer or cocktails — as well as summer pique-niques and barbecues. While plain thin-cut and ridge-cut potato chips (chips à l'ancienne and chips paysannes) remain staples, flavoured Breton chips (Comté, Chèvre & Piment d'Espelette, Camembert, Poulet Rôti) and corn tortilla chips served with salsa or guacamole have driven the 42% ten-year surge in French chip consumption.

  • Pique-niques & outdoor summer dining
  • Tortilla chips with salsa & guacamole at parties
  • Beach & vacation snacking
