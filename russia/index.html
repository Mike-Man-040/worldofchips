Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Russia, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during dacha weekends. Other popular snacking occasions include beer gatherings, train provisions, football viewing. The average bag of chips costs ₽120/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

  • Dacha weekends
  • Beer gatherings
  • Train provisions
  • Football viewing
