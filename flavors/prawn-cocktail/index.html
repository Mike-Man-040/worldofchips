Prawn cocktail crisps are a sweet, tangy, tomato-and-seafood-sauce flavour and a fixture of British crisp aisles.

Origin & History

Walkers introduced Prawn Cocktail in the 1970s, inspired by the prawn cocktail starter that was fashionable at the time. As of 2010 it ranked fourth among Walkers’ flavours, after Cheese & Onion, Ready Salted and Salt & Vinegar. Ireland’s Tayto also sells a Prawn Cocktail crisp.
