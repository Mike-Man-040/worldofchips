Chip Seasoning Powders for Homemade Crisps

Factory chips are seasoned in a tumbling drum straight after frying. At home, the equivalent is to toss warm chips in a bowl with the seasoning; BBC Food says to season crisps while they’re still warm. FAQs

When should I season homemade chips?

While they are still warm. BBC Food says to season crisps while they’re warm, and Food52 tosses the chips in a large bowl with the seasoning, flipping them to coat.

How do I make salt and vinegar chips at home?

Liquid vinegar makes chips soggy, so use a vinegar powder or a salt & vinegar seasoning and toss the chips while they are warm.

What seasonings suit air-fryer crisps?

BBC Good Food suggests barbecue rub, garlic granules or paprika, tossed with the oiled slices before cooking.
