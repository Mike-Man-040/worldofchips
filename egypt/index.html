Potato Chips in Egypt

Egypt's potato chip market is valued at $0.7B and grows at 6.0% per year. Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Egypt, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during street food pairing. Other popular snacking occasions include ramadan iftar snacking, football viewing, beach snacking. The average bag of chips costs EGP 15/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

  • Street food pairing
  • Ramadan iftar snacking
  • Football viewing
  • Beach snacking

Did You Know?

In Egypt, Lay's is known as 'Chipsy' — the name has become the generic word for any potato chip.
