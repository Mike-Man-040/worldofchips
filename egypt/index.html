Potato Chips in Egypt

Africa

In Egypt, "chipsy" (شيبسي) is not just a brand name — it is the everyday Arabic noun for any bag of potato chips. That status goes back to Chipsy for Food Industries, founded in 1982 and acquired by PepsiCo in 2001. Operating a 110,000-square-metre plant in Sixth of October City — where PepsiCo Egypt inaugurated a new $20 million, 25,000-tonne production line in February 2023 across nine lines — Chipsy holds roughly 60% of Egypt's snack market and exports to 11 countries across Africa, Asia and the Middle East. Following an agricultural seed programme started in 2016, 100% of Chipsy's potatoes are grown locally on Egyptian farms. Its largest domestic rival is Tiger, the flagship flat and ridged potato-chip brand of Egypt Foods Group, while Egyptian craft maker Spuds caters to the premium kettle-cooked segment with flavours like Sea Salt, Prime Ribs and Worcestershire Sauce. Across Egyptian kiosks (kushks) and supermarkets, the flavour hierarchy is unmistakably local: Kebab (charcoal-grilled meat and onion spice), Shatta & Lemon (spicy red chili with sharp citrus), Tomato (spiced ketchup-tomato), Salt & Vinegar and Cheese lead both the Chipsy and Tiger ranges.

Top Potato Chip Brands in Egypt

PepsiCo Egypt's Chipsy (founded 1982 in Sixth of October City) leads with roughly 60% of Egypt's snack market, competing head-to-head in neighbourhood kushks (kiosks) and supermarkets with Egypt Foods Group's Tiger (and sister brands Big Chips and Excellence), local kettle-chip maker Spuds, Doritos and Pringles.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Egypt, single-serve bags of Chipsy or Tiger (averaging around EGP 15) are bought daily at street-corner kiosks (kushks) to eat alongside foul and ta'meya sandwiches or bowls of koshary, shared late at night after Ramadan iftar and during suhoor, or crunched in cafés during Al Ahly, Zamalek and Egyptian national-team football matches.

  • Pairing with koshary, foul & ta'meya sandwiches
  • Ramadan post-iftar & suhoor gatherings
  • Football viewing at cafés & home
  • Mediterranean North Coast beach snacking

Did You Know?

Founded in 1982, PepsiCo Egypt's Chipsy holds around 60% of the country's snack market, sources 100% of its potatoes from Egyptian farms, and is so ubiquitous that Egyptians call any bag of potato chips 'chipsy'.
