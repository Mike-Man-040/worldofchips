A snack box is an easy Christmas present for someone who already has everything: a parcel of snacks from Japan, Mexico, Korea or a new country every month. The catch is that the boxes differ a lot on price, contents and, above all, where they ship. Universal Yums, MexiCrate and SnackCrate only deliver in North America, while the Japanese and Korean boxes ship worldwide. Prices are in US dollars per box before shipping unless noted, and offers change often, especially around the holidays, so check at checkout.

🎁 Quick Picks

  • Cheapest box: MexiCrate Pequeña at $14.99, or Universal Yums from $18 a box (both US/Canada only).
  • Most premium-feeling gift: Bokksu: traditional Japanese snacks plus tea.
  • Best for chip lovers: TokyoTreat (a chips and salty treats category) or Daebak (chips and crackers in every box).

  • A new country every month: Universal Yums or SnackCrate.
  • Sending to Europe? Bokksu, TokyoTreat, Sakuraco and Daebak ship there; Daebak includes VAT and duties.

Ordering a Snack Box as a Christmas Gift

  • Check where it ships first. Universal Yums ships only to the US and Canada, MexiCrate only to the US and Canada, and SnackCrate only within the US (plus US territories and military bases or embassies).
  • Allow time. TokyoTreat and Sakuraco quote 7–28 days after dispatch, and Daebak says a first box usually arrives within 15–20 days. MexiCrate ships US orders within 2 business days, with 2–7 days in transit. None of the boxes published a guaranteed Christmas delivery cut-off that we could find, so check at checkout.
  • Watch for customs. Daebak includes VAT and duties. TokyoTreat, Sakuraco and Bokksu warn that EU buyers may owe customs duty on delivery.
  • Gift options. SnackCrate has a “Give a gift” page; for the other boxes, check each site for gift messages and prepaid plans.

FAQs

Which snack box is best as a Christmas gift?

For a premium-feeling present, Bokksu (traditional Japanese snacks and tea, $32.99–$39.99 a box). For full-size modern Japanese snacks and chips, TokyoTreat ($32.50–$37.50). For a cheaper gift in the US or Canada, MexiCrate starts at $14.99 and Universal Yums at $18 a box.

Which snack boxes ship outside North America?

Bokksu, TokyoTreat, Sakuraco and Daebak ship internationally, including to Europe. Universal Yums and MexiCrate ship only to the US and Canada, and SnackCrate ships within the US, US territories and to military bases and embassies.

What is the cheapest international snack box?

MexiCrate’s Pequeña box costs $14.99 (10–15 Mexican candies and snacks). Universal Yums’ smallest Yum Box costs $18–$20 a box with free shipping in the contiguous US.

Is SnackCrate the same as Universal Yums?

No. They are separate companies with a similar idea: a different country every month. SnackCrate says its snacks are imported from the featured country and each crate includes a local drink. Universal Yums ships to the US and Canada, while SnackCrate ships within the US.

Do snack box prices include shipping?

Mostly not. Universal Yums ships free in the contiguous US and Daebak’s shipping fee includes VAT and duties, but Bokksu, TokyoTreat and Sakuraco charge shipping per box. MexiCrate offers free US shipping on orders over $75.
