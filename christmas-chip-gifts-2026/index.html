FAQs

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves chips?

A country set of chips they can’t buy locally, such as Korean Honey Butter Chip or British Monster Munch, or a snack subscription box from Japan or Korea. For a ready-to-gift present, the Broadway Basketeers tower arrives assembled with a gift tag.

Which snack subscription box is best as a gift?

Bokksu feels the most premium for Japan fans, TokyoTreat suits modern Japanese snacks, Daebak is the Korea pick and Universal Yums sends a different country each month (US and Canada only). See our snack box comparison for plans and shipping.

Are there chip gifts under $20?

Yes. Haitai Honey Butter Chips (3 bags) was $14.50 on Amazon when we checked on 22 October 2026, and Universal Yums starts from $18 a month. Prices change, so check before you buy.

When should I order chip gifts for Christmas?

Imported snacks and subscription boxes ship from abroad, so order early and check each seller’s holiday delivery dates at checkout.
