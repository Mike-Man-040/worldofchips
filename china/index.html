Potato Chips in China

Asia

China's potato-chip story is largely a Lay's story. PepsiCo brought Lay's (乐事, "happy things") to China in 1993, planted its first high-quality potato seedlings in a desert region that year, set up China's first self-operated potato farm in Baotou, Inner Mongolia, in 1998, and opened its first food factory in Songjiang, Shanghai, in 2000. From the start the brand localised hard. By 2004 Shanghai shoppers could buy Cool Cucumber (清怡黄瓜味), Fresh Lemon and Hokkaido Seaweed chips, plus a "Chinese Favorites" range with Spicy Crab, Peking Duck and Five Spice Fish. One count puts the number of Lay's flavours sold in China at more than 200. Original, BBQ and tomato remain the everyday classics, while newer launches include spicy crayfish, cumin lamb skewer and a wasabi-style mustard flavour. Lay's is cited as holding close to half of China's potato-chip market.

Top Potato Chip Brands in China

PepsiCo's Lay's leads China's potato-chip market, cited at close to 50% share, with a range split into bagged, canned (Lay's Stax), wavy and "natural" lines. Pringles is the main canned rival, and regional snack makers compete on price.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In China, chips are bought in convenience stores and online. On JD.com alone, Lay's best-selling item in 2022 was a tomato, original and BBQ combo pack, which sold 860,000 units. Limited editions and local-cuisine flavours keep shoppers trying new bags.

FAQ: Potato Chips in China

What are the most popular potato chip brands in China?

The leading potato chip brands in China are Lay's, Pringles and Oishi, with Lay's at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about ¥8/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in China?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in China are Original, Cucumber, Tomato and BBQ. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Spicy Crayfish, Cumin Lamb Skewer, Peking Duck, Spicy Crab and Wasabi Mustard. Chips are typically eaten during late-night & internet café snacking, hot pot accompaniment and livestream snack hauls.
