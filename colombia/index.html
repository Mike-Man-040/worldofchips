Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Colombia, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during onces (afternoon snack). Other popular snacking occasions include party accompaniment, bus travel, street food pairing. The average bag of chips costs COP 3,500/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Colombia

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Colombia?

The leading potato chip brands in Colombia are Margarita (Lay's), Super Ricas and De Todito, with Margarita (Lay's) at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about COP 3,500/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Colombia?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Colombia are Natural, Limón, BBQ and Pollo. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Chicharrón, Hogao and Guasacaca. Chips are typically eaten during onces (afternoon snack), party accompaniment and bus travel.
