Potato Chips in Sweden

Europe

Sweden's potato chip market is valued at $0.5B and grows at 2.0% per year. With per capita consumption of 1.8 kg and an average bag price of 35 SEK/bag, Sweden has developed its own distinctive chip culture shaped by local culinary traditions and snacking habits. The market features both international giants and beloved local brands, each offering flavors that reflect the country's unique food heritage. From Sourcream & Onion to the more adventurous Smörstekt Lök, Sweden's chip scene offers a fascinating window into local taste preferences and snacking culture.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Sweden, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during fredagsmys (cozy friday). Other popular snacking occasions include midsommar celebrations, football & hockey viewing, summer bbq & crayfish party. The average bag of chips costs 35 SEK/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Sweden

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Sweden?

The leading potato chip brands in Sweden are OLW, Estrella, Pringles and Lay's, with OLW at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. Sweden's chip market is listed at $0.5B, with an average bag price of 35 SEK/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Sweden?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Sweden are Sourcream & Onion, Dill & Gräddfil, Grill and Salt. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Smörstekt Lök, Räkor (Shrimp), Västkustsallad and Kebab.
