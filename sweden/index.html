Potato Chips in Sweden

Europe

Sweden's chip aisle is a long-running duel between two home-grown brands. Estrella says it was first: in 1957 founder Folke Andersson, until then a banana importer, tasted potato chips at a boxing match in New York and the same year started Sweden's first chip and popcorn production in Alingsås, before moving to Kortedala in 1964 and to Angered, Gothenburg, in 1983. Its rival OLW was born out of concern for jobs. In 1967 the crispbread maker Wasabröd teamed up with America's Borden to form Old London Wasa in Filipstad, Värmland, and the factory started making cheez doodles and then potato chips in 1968; the name was shortened to OLW in 1975, and Norway's Orkla has owned it since 2005. Estrella has been part of Germany's Intersnack since 2014. Swedes' favourites are remarkably stable: Estrella launched Grillchips in 1967 and Sourcream & Onion in 1988, which it calls Sweden's best-selling chip, while OLW names Lättsaltade, Dill & Gräslök, Grill and Sourcream & Onion as the four all-time favourite flavours. Estrella says it ships 37 million bags a year to Swedish shops, about 70 bags a minute.

Top Potato Chip Brands in Sweden

Two brands dominate Swedish chips: OLW (Orkla, made in Filipstad since 1968) and Estrella (Intersnack, made in Angered, Gothenburg). Both sell the same classic line-up of Sourcream & Onion, Grill and dill chips, plus cheese puffs, nuts and dip mixes. Pringles and Lay's are the main international names on the shelf.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Sweden, chips belong to fredagsmys, the cosy Friday night in with family or friends; OLW built its advertising around the idea from 2009. Bags of Sourcream & Onion come with a bowl of dip made from a powder mix, and Estrella recommends its dill chips with crayfish at an August kräftskiva.

Did You Know?

Estrella founder Folke Andersson first tasted potato chips at a boxing match in New York in 1957. Before snacks he imported bananas from South America, which is why the brand is called Estrella (Spanish for "star"), and a football stadium in Ecuador still carries his name.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Sweden

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Sweden?

The leading potato chip brands in Sweden are OLW, Estrella, Pringles and Lay's, with OLW at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about 35 SEK/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Sweden?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Sweden are Sourcream & Onion, Grill, Dill & Gräslök and Lättsaltade (Lightly Salted). For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Dill & Gräslök (Dill & Chive), Grillchips, Chili Cream Cheese and Balsamico Salt & Peppar. Chips are typically eaten during fredagsmys (cosy Friday night in), kräftskiva (crayfish parties) and chips with dip mix.
