Spicy chips are one of the fastest ways to taste how different countries do heat: Mexico’s chilli-and-lime, American hot-sauce flavours and jalapeño kettle chips. FAQs

What are the spiciest Takis?

On the Amazon listings we checked, Takis rates both Fuego (chilli pepper and lime) and Blue Heat (hot chilli pepper) as Extreme on its own Heat-O-Meter. The Blue Heat listing describes it as a flavour that battles for the brand’s hottest snack.

Are Takis potato chips?

Classic Takis are rolled corn tortilla chips made by Barcel, Grupo Bimbo’s snack arm. Takis also sells stackable potato crisps in cans, such as Fuego Crisps and Blue Heat Crisps, with the same seasonings.

Is there a Scoville rating for spicy chips?

Not for the chips in this guide. None of the makers publishes a Scoville figure for these chips, so we quote each brand’s own heat description instead of inventing a ranking.

What is a milder spicy chip to start with?

Cape Cod Sweet & Spicy Jalapeño pairs jalapeño with a sweet touch on kettle-cooked chips. Pringles x Hot Ones Rojo uses medium-spice jalapeño and habanero hot-sauce flavours.
