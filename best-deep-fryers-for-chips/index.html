Frying Safety

  • Dry the slices thoroughly. BBC Food warns that hot oil will spit unless the potatoes are as dry as possible.
  • Fry in small batches so the chips don’t stick together (Food52), and never leave hot oil unattended (BBC Good Food).

FAQs

What temperature should I fry homemade chips at?

Around 180 °C (350 °F). That is the frying temperature in Food52’s homemade potato chip recipe and in BBC Good Food’s deep-fried matchstick chips.

How long do thin crisps take to fry?

Very little time. Food52 says a minute or two, until the bubbling calms, and BBC Food warns that very thin slices can cook in 10 to 15 seconds, so watch them closely.

Do I need a deep fryer?

No. A heavy pan and a thermometer work, but a thermostat-controlled fryer holds the temperature more easily. An air fryer is a lower-oil alternative.
