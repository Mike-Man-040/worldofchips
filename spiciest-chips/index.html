FAQs

What is the spiciest chip ever sold?

The Paqui One Chip Challenge chip, made with Carolina Reaper (and later Naga Viper) peppers, was marketed as one of the hottest. Paqui withdrew it in September 2023 after a teenager died, so it is no longer sold.

Do spicy chips have a Scoville rating?

Makers don’t publish Scoville ratings for their chips. Scoville figures on this page describe the peppers used, such as the jalapeño or ghost pepper, not the finished chip.

Are Takis hotter than Flamin’ Hot chips?

Takis rates Fuego and Blue Heat as Extreme on its own Heat-O-Meter. We rank them above Ruffles Flamin’ Hot, but that is our editorial judgement, not a lab measurement.
