Haitai’s Honey Butter Chip, the honey-and-butter potato chip that became a Korean craze in 2014, is not a supermarket item in Sweden, the Netherlands or Germany. You find it at Asian grocers and Korean online shops, usually in the 60 g bag. Below are the shops we found listing it when we checked on 3 November 2026. Prices include VAT; shipping is extra.

🇸🇪 Honey Butter Chip in Sweden

We could not confirm a Swedish shop selling Haitai Honey Butter Chip when we checked. The simplest option is an EU shop that delivers to Sweden, so there is no customs to pay: Nanuko (€2.79, delivers across the EU) or K-shop (€2.95, ships to EU countries). Shipping is charged on top, so it pays to order several bags or other Korean snacks at once. Asian supermarkets in Swedish cities may carry it too; ask in store.

Honey Butter Chip on Amazon

On Amazon.com (the US store) the Haitai Honey Butter Chips 3-pack was $14.50 and in stock on 22 October 2026. It ships from the US, so check delivery to your country and import fees at checkout; since 1 July 2026 EU parcels from outside the EU may be charged customs duty.

FAQs

Can you buy Honey Butter Chip in the Netherlands?

Yes, at Asian grocers. Tjin’s Toko in Amsterdam listed the 60 g bag at €3.20 when we checked on 3 November 2026, and the Amazing Oriental chain lists it in its range.

Where can I buy Honey Butter Chip in Germany?

Online Asian shops such as Nanuko (€2.79), K-shop (€2.95, with a store in Frankfurt), MAOMAO and World of Sweets (€3.49 each) listed the 60 g bag on 3 November 2026.

Is Honey Butter Chip sold in Sweden?

We could not confirm a Swedish stockist. German shops such as Nanuko and K-shop deliver to Sweden from inside the EU, so no customs duty applies.
