Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Brazil, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during churrasco accompaniment. Other popular snacking occasions include beach kiosk snacking, football viewing, bar & boteco culture. The average bag of chips costs R$8/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

FAQ: Potato Chips in Brazil

What are the most popular potato chip brands in Brazil?

The leading potato chip brands in Brazil are Ruffles, Lay's, Pringles and Elma Chips, with Ruffles at the top. They compete for shelf space in supermarkets, convenience stores and online. An average bag costs about R$8/bag.

What potato chip flavors are popular in Brazil?

The bestselling, most widely available flavors in Brazil are Cheddar, Churrasco, Original and Sour Cream & Onion. For something more distinctive, look for local specialities such as Feijoada, Picanha, Steak & Onion and Coxinha. Chips are typically eaten during churrasco accompaniment, beach kiosk snacking and football viewing.
