Potato Chips in Brazil

South America

Brazil's potato chip market is valued at $1.5B and grows at 4.8% per year. With per capita consumption of 0.5 kg and an average bag price of R$8/bag, Brazil has developed its own distinctive chip culture shaped by local culinary traditions and snacking habits. The market features both international giants and beloved local brands, each offering flavors that reflect the country's unique food heritage. From Cheddar to the more adventurous Feijoada, Brazil's chip scene offers a fascinating window into local taste preferences and snacking culture.

Top Potato Chip Brands in Brazil

The Brazil chip market is led by Ruffles, followed by Lay's and Pringles, Elma Chips. These brands compete for shelf space across supermarkets, convenience stores, and increasingly through online retail channels.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Brazil, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during churrasco accompaniment. Other popular snacking occasions include beach kiosk snacking, football viewing, bar & boteco culture. The average bag of chips costs R$8/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

  • Churrasco accompaniment
  • Beach kiosk snacking
  • Football viewing
  • Bar & boteco culture
