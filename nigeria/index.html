Potato Chips in Nigeria

Africa

Nigeria's potato chip market is valued at $0.3B and grows at 9.5% per year. The market features both international giants and beloved local brands, each offering flavors that reflect the country's unique food heritage. From Original to the more adventurous Jollof, Nigeria's chip scene offers a fascinating window into local taste preferences and snacking culture.

Snacking Culture & Occasions

In Nigeria, potato chips are most commonly enjoyed during party jollof accompaniment. Other popular snacking occasions include market snacking, school canteen, owambe celebrations. The average bag of chips costs ₦500/bag, making them an accessible everyday snack across all demographics.

  • Party jollof accompaniment
  • Market snacking
  • School canteen
  • Owambe celebrations
